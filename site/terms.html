2. We never take custody

At no point do we hold, control, move, or have the ability to recover your funds. Specifically:

  • We never hold your private keys, seed phrase, or wallet credentials, and we will never ask for them. Anyone who does is attempting to steal from you.
  • Deposit addresses shown on the Site are generated by the third-party routing network, not by us. We do not control them and cannot access funds sent to them.
  • On the Cosmos route, every transaction is signed by you in your own wallet. We cannot sign, initiate, or reverse anything on your behalf.
  • Where the Site offers to read an address from your wallet, that is a read-only convenience. We never request a signature for that purpose.

Because we never take custody, we are not able to reverse, cancel, refund, or recover a transfer — not as a matter of policy, but because no such ability exists.

3. The third parties that do the work

The Site does not itself bridge, swap, or transmit anything. It is an interface to independent networks and protocols, each operated by parties unconnected to us:

  • NEAR Intents (also referred to as 1Click or Defuse) — used for transfers from Ethereum and other EVM chains, Bitcoin, Tron, XRP, TON and the other non-Cosmos networks listed in the interface. It generates the deposit addresses and its solvers perform the conversion and delivery.
  • Skip:Go (Cosmos Labs) — used to plan and execute transfers from Cosmos networks.
  • Circle CCTP and Noble — used to settle USDC into Solana on the Cosmos route.
  • Keplr, and any Solana wallet you choose to connect — wallet software operated by its own publisher.

4. Transactions are final

Blockchain transactions are irreversible. Once you send funds, neither we nor anyone else can undo it. In particular:

  • Funds sent on the wrong network, or to an address on a different network from the one shown, are generally unrecoverable.
  • A deposit address is tied to a single quoted transfer. Sending to it twice, or reusing an old address, may result in a transfer filling at an outdated rate or not filling at all.
  • Sending an amount below the stated minimum may leave a transfer incomplete.
  • If a transfer cannot complete, the routing network may return funds to the refund address you supplied. If that address is wrong or one you do not control, those funds are lost.

5. Quotes, rates and fees

Amounts shown before you send are estimates supplied by third-party solvers and move with the market. The final amount you receive may be lower. Where a guaranteed minimum is displayed, that figure is the routing network’s commitment, not ours.

A service fee of 0.5% is included in the quoted amounts shown on the Site. On the Cosmos route this fee is not applied to transfers under $20. Network fees, relay fees, solver spread, and — the first time a wallet receives a given token on Solana — a one-time token account rent of roughly $0.19 are charged by the networks themselves and are additional to, and outside, that service fee. Fees may change; the figure shown in the interface at the time of your transfer is the one that applies.
