Cosmos — Skip affiliate

One payment per fee-bearing leg, paid in USDC on Osmosis to . Each row is a completed transfer.

Why this understates volume. The affiliate cut rides the Osmosis swap. A transfer with no swap in it — USDC that is already USDC, moving to Noble and out — has nothing for a fee to attach to, so it moves real money and earns nothing. Stablecoin holders are the clearest case, and they are exactly who the sweep serves best.

NEAR — 1Click appFees

Credited as an intents balance held by the intents.near contract for . A normal NEAR wallet shows these settlements as “App Interaction — execute_intents” and no balance change: the funds are real, but a wallet cannot display a balance the contract holds on your behalf.

You keep half of what you charge. We submit appFees: 50 and 1Click rewrites it to 25 bps for us and 25 bps for them. The user is charged 0.5%; we receive 0.25%. The volume figures above are derived from what reached us, so they already account for it — an earlier version did not, and read half.

What is not in these numbers

Network gas and the CCTP smart relay charge (a flat ~$0.19 to leave Cosmos, whatever the size) are costs of the route. The user pays them to validators and relayers. None of it reaches us, and none of it appears above.
